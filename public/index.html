How to play

The game master knows the full story. Work it out by asking questions that can be answered with yes, no, or irrelevant.

Ask questions

  • Keep to one yes/no question per message. "Did the man die of natural causes?" "Is the location important?"
  • "Irrelevant" means the detail does not matter. "Can't say" means the answer would give too much away or the story does not decide it.

Get a hint

  • Pick Get a hint and send, or type /hint.
  • Hints are progressive: each one gives away a little more.

Submit a solution

  • Pick Submit a solution and describe what happened, or type /guess followed by your answer. "/guess They are goldfish and the cat knocked over the bowl"
  • If you are close, the game master says so and you can try again or ask for the solution.

Give up

  • Pick Give up: show solution or type /solution. You will be asked to confirm.
  • The puzzle is then marked as revealed. You can reset it from the question bank.

Game master help

  • With the switch under the chat on (default), the game master may add a short clarification to an answer, suggest what to ask when your message is not a yes/no question, and point at what is missing in a close guess.
  • Switch it off for strict play: bare yes / no / irrelevant / can't say answers and verdicts with no pointers. Hints still work when you ask for them.
