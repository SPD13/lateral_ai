How the score is calculated

Only puzzles you have solved count, and each one counts once. A solved puzzle is worth points for its difficulty, minus what it took you to get there:

  • Easy 2 points · Medium 3 points · Hard 4 points
  • Each hint used: −0.5
  • Each question answered: −0.1
  • Each solution submitted: −0.2

A puzzle can never score below zero, so a long hunt costs you the puzzle's points but never subtracts from the rest. Puzzles you revealed or are still working on score nothing, and a puzzle deleted from the bank stops counting.

Example: a hard puzzle solved with 6 questions, 1 hint and 2 tries scores 4 − 0.6 − 0.5 − 0.4 = 2.5 points.
